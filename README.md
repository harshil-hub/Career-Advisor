# Personalized Career & Education Advisor

A web-based application that provides personalized career and education recommendations based on a student's interests, skills, and academic performance.

## Overview

The Personalized Career & Education Advisor is designed to help students explore suitable career paths and educational opportunities based on their individual profiles.

## Features

- Personalized career recommendations
- Education pathway suggestions
- Interest and skill-based recommendations
- CGPA-based guidance
- Simple and user-friendly interface
- Responsive web design

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript


## How It Works

1. Enter your basic details.
2. Select your department and areas of interest.
3. Enter your skills and CGPA.
4. Click **Get Recommendation**.
5. View personalized career and education suggestions.
